## 📘 What the subject covers

> **Física (780000)** · 6 ECTS · 1st year · 1st semester · contents from the 2024-25 teaching guide · [official subject page](https://www.uah.es/es/estudios/estudios-oficiales/grados/asignatura/Fisica-780000/)

| Unit | Hours | Topics |
|---|---|---|
| Electrostatics in vacuum and in conductors | 12 h | Electric field and potential · Conductors in electrostatic equilibrium |
| Energy of charge distributions | 2 h | Energy stored in the electrostatic field |
| Electric current and DC circuits | 5 h | Microscopic basis of current · Direct-current circuit analysis |
| Magnetostatics and electromagnetic induction | 12 h | Magnetic fields of currents · Faraday's and Lenz's laws |
| Fields in material media | 8 h | Dielectrics · Magnetic materials |
| AC circuits and electromagnetic waves | 8 h | Alternating-current circuits · Electromagnetic waves |
| Lab | 9 h | Experimental data analysis and error propagation · Oscilloscope · DC circuits · Electromagnetic induction |
